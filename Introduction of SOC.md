# Security Operations Center (SOC) – Introduction

## What is SOC?

**SOC (Security Operations Center)** is a centralized team responsible for **monitoring, detecting, investigating, and responding to cybersecurity threats** within an organization's IT infrastructure.

The SOC team works continuously to protect systems, networks, servers, and applications from cyber attacks such as malware, phishing, unauthorized access, and data breaches.

---

## What is a SOC Analyst?

A **SOC Analyst** is a cybersecurity professional responsible for:

* Monitoring security alerts and logs
* Investigating suspicious activities
* Analyzing potential security threats
* Responding to security incidents

Their primary goal is to **detect threats early and minimize damage to the organization**.

---

## SOC Analyst Roles

### SOC Analyst L1 (Tier 1 – Monitoring)

This is an **entry-level role** in the SOC.

**Responsibilities:**

* Monitor security alerts from SIEM tools
* Perform initial investigation of alerts
* Identify false positives
* Conduct basic triage of security events
* Escalate suspicious incidents to L2 analysts

---

### SOC Analyst L2 (Tier 2 – Investigation)

L2 analysts perform **deeper investigation and analysis of incidents**.

**Responsibilities:**

* Investigate complex security incidents
* Perform malware analysis
* Conduct endpoint investigations
* Use threat intelligence to analyze attacks
* Support incident response activities

---

### SOC Analyst L3 (Tier 3 – Advanced Security)

L3 analysts are **security experts responsible for advanced threat detection and strategy**.

**Responsibilities:**

* Perform advanced threat hunting
* Analyze attack patterns and adversary behavior
* Lead incident response activities
* Improve SOC detection rules and security controls
* Develop strategies to prevent future attacks

---

## SOC Workflow

1. An attacker attempts unauthorized access (e.g., login attempt).
2. The SIEM system generates a security alert.
3. **L1 Analyst** reviews the alert and performs initial analysis.
4. If suspicious activity is detected, the incident is **escalated to L2**.
5. **L2 Analyst** performs deep investigation and confirms whether it is an attack.
6. If the attack is confirmed, the incident is **contained and escalated to L3**.
7. **L3 Analyst** performs threat hunting, root cause analysis, and prevention measures.
