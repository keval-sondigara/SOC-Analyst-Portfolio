# What is Alert Reporting?

- when SOC Analyst investigates alerts and make it's proper documentation/report is called Alert Reporting.

---

# What is Alert Escalation?

- When L1 Analyst can not resolve alert or may have some serious threat then L1 escalates that alert to L2,L3 or incident response team is called Alert Escalation.

---

# Escalation Steps

1) recives an alert from SIEM.
2) do initial triage.
3) analyze the logs.
4) determine the severity.
5) escalate it if anything confirmed malicious.
6) create the ticket.
7) assign it to L2/Inclident Response team.
